LIBER V · TRUST WITHOUT SANITIZATION

The machinery is allowed to be visible.

Transparency here is not a corporate disclaimer. It is part of the aesthetic: material, transformation, operator, instrument and uncertainty remain separable.

01

AI assisted.

AI may assist analysis, sequencing, code, visual systems, language or variant generation. It does not silently become the artist, an autonomous authority or a source of observed facts.

02

Human governed.

Release, acceptance, rejection, sequencing and public execution remain human decisions. The system can propose; it does not inherit authorship by automation.

03

SFI observed.

System Friction Institute can retain provenance, longitudinal return and epistemic distinctions. Observation does not make SFI the artistic owner of KXTXR.
